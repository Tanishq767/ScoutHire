About ScoutHire

ScoutHire is an intelligent campus recruitment platform designed to connect companies, colleges, and students throughout the recruitment lifecycle. Companies can create recruitment drives and evaluate applicants, colleges can approve companies and drives for their institution, and students can discover and apply to approved opportunities.
